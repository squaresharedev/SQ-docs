<AnswerBlock>

Copy your storefront's snippet from its Share page in the dashboard and paste it into your website where the storefront should appear. Before the Copy button works, turn on Show on my website, add your domain to Allowed domains and save. The storefront then shows on your published https site.

</AnswerBlock>

## What do I need before I start?

- A Square Share account at [dashboard.squareshare.eu](https://dashboard.squareshare.eu).
- At least one product added to the dashboard.
- A storefront created in the dashboard with at least one product on it.
- Seller details complete (name, address, and contact email).
- Product pages turned on in the storefront designer. A click on a storefront tile opens the product's page.
- A website served over **https**. The storefront will not show on http pages.

[Open the dashboard](https://dashboard.squareshare.eu)

</Buttons>

## How do I add the storefront?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, select **Storefront** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Share** on your storefront's card (or **Share** in the designer header if the storefront is open). The page that opens is titled "Share this storefront".
3. Under "Choose the sites it shows on", turn on **Show on my website**.
4. Type your site's domain in **Allowed domains** (for example, `yoursite.com`). You can add up to 10 domains, comma-separated. `www.` variants are handled automatically; subdomains must be listed separately.
5. Click **Save settings**.
6. Under "Paste the snippet into your site", choose your site builder from the **Your site builder** dropdown.
7. Select the **Script** tab (or **Iframe only** if your site strips scripts).
8. Click **Copy embed snippet**.
9. Paste the snippet into your page (see "Where do I paste the snippet?" below).
10. Under "Add this to your privacy policy" (Required), click **Copy privacy text** and add that text to your site's privacy policy.

</Steps>

The snippet the dashboard copies for you looks like this (the dashboard fills in your real key):

<Snippet>

```html title="Script snippet"
<div data-squareshare-storefront="YOUR-EMBED-KEY"></div>
<script async src="https://embed.squareshare.eu/widget.js"></script>
```

</Snippet>

Replace `YOUR-EMBED-KEY` with the key shown on the Share page in your dashboard. The dashboard's **Copy embed snippet** button fills this in for you automatically.

## Where do I paste the snippet?

Choose your site builder:

| Site builder | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Another site | Paste it into your page's HTML where the storefront should appear. |
| [WordPress](/en/site-builders/wordpress) | Add a Custom HTML block where the storefront should appear, and paste it in. |
| [Squarespace](/en/site-builders/squarespace) | Add a Code block and paste it in. If the storefront only appears after a reload, switch to Iframe only. |
| [Wix](/en/site-builders/wix) | Add Embed Code, then Embed HTML, and paste it in. Then drag the box as tall as your storefront. |
| [Webflow](/en/site-builders/webflow) | Add a Code Embed element and paste it in. It shows on your published site. |
| Shopify | In the theme editor, add a Custom Liquid section and paste it in. |
| Google Sites | Choose Insert, then Embed, then Embed code, and paste it in. |
| [Framer](/en/site-builders/framer) | Add an Embed component, set it to HTML, and paste it in. |
| [Carrd](/en/site-builders/carrd) | Add an Embed element, set its type to Code, and paste it in. |

Step-by-step guides for each builder, with plan needs and fixes, are under [Site builders](/en/site-builders).

## Which snippet should I use?

| Snippet | Best for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Script | Most sites | Sizes itself to fit the storefront's content. Requires the host page to allow scripts. |
| Iframe only | Sites that strip or block scripts | Fixed height of 640 px. Scrolls inside the frame. Use this on Squarespace if the Script version has problems. |

The iframe snippet, for sites that strip scripts:

<Snippet>

```html title="Iframe snippet"
<iframe
  src="https://embed.squareshare.eu/e/YOUR-EMBED-KEY/yoursite.com/www.yoursite.com"
  title="Shop"
  loading="lazy"
  sandbox="allow-scripts allow-same-origin allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox allow-forms"
  referrerpolicy="no-referrer"
  style="display:block;width:100%;height:640px;border:0"
></iframe>
```

</Snippet>

The address ends with your saved domains and their `www.` versions (up to four). The dashboard fills in your key and domains when you click **Copy embed snippet** on the **Iframe only** tab. Copy it again after you change your domains.

## What do I add to my privacy policy?

The following paragraph is required. Copy it from the dashboard's **Copy privacy text** button and add it to your site's privacy policy:

> This site shows a storefront provided by Square Share. When it loads, the visitor's IP address and browser details are sent to Square Share to display the products and to count visits. Square Share sets no cookies and stores nothing on the visitor's device for this. Details: https://squareshare.eu/legal/privacy-policy/

The dashboard fills in that link to Square Share's privacy policy when you copy the text.

## How do I check it worked?

There is no test button. To confirm the storefront is showing:

1. Open your published website (not the site builder's editor preview) in a browser.
2. Go to the page where you pasted the snippet.
3. The storefront should appear.

If the storefront does not show, check these points:

- Your site's address must be in **Allowed domains** and must be saved.
- The page must be served over https, not http.
- Code pasted into a site builder shows on the published site, not in the builder's editor.
- To test on a preview address (such as `yoursite.webflow.io`), add that address to **Allowed domains** as well.

A refused embed draws nothing visible on the page. The Script version logs a console message starting with `[Square Share] The storefront is not showing on` to help with diagnosis.
