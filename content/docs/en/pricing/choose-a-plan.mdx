<AnswerBlock>

It depends on what you sell through Square Share checkout in a month. Under €400, Free costs least. From €400 to €1,800, Starter does. Above €1,800, Pro does, and the more you sell, the further ahead it gets. Early access sellers are on Pro free for six months, so the choice matters once that ends.

## How do I work out my cost?

Add the plan's monthly price to its share of what you sold through Square Share checkout that month:

| Plan | Cost in a month |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 5% of your checkout sales |
| Starter | €12 plus 2% of your checkout sales |
| Pro | €39 plus 0.5% of your checkout sales |

Sales through your own purchase link or by email do not count: no share is taken on them, on any plan.

## Where does each plan pull ahead?

- **Starter costs less than Free above €400 a month.** At €400, the 3 percentage points of share that Starter saves are worth exactly its €12.
- **Pro costs less than Starter above €1,800 a month.** At €1,800, the 1.5 percentage points that Pro saves are worth the €27 between the two prices.
- **Pro costs less than Free above about €867 a month.** Useful if you want Pro's limits or tools anyway.

## What does each plan cost at my sales?

Before VAT and Stripe's card fee. The cheapest plan is in bold.

| Checkout sales in a month | Free | Starter | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| €100 | **€5.00** | €14.00 | €39.50 |
| €250 | **€12.50** | €17.00 | €40.25 |
| €400 | **€20.00** | **€20.00** | €41.00 |
| €500 | €25.00 | **€22.00** | €41.50 |
| €1,000 | €50.00 | **€32.00** | €44.00 |
| €1,800 | €90.00 | **€48.00** | **€48.00** |
| €2,500 | €125.00 | €62.00 | **€51.50** |
| €5,000 | €250.00 | €112.00 | **€64.00** |
| €10,000 | €500.00 | €212.00 | **€89.00** |

At €5,000 a month, Pro comes to €64 in all: 1.3% of those sales. Free would take €250.

Shares are rounded to the cent on each sale, so a real month can differ from these figures by a few cents. Stripe's card fee is the same on every plan, so it does not change which plan is cheapest.

## When is price not the only reason?

Pick by what you need, too:

| If you need | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| More than 20 products or 3 storefronts | Starter (60 and 10) or Pro (500 and unlimited) |
| More than 2 people on the store, you included | Starter (5) or Pro (unlimited) |
| New features before everyone else | Starter or Pro |
| AI writing help, analytics reports or priority support | Pro |

What every plan includes, and what the paid plans add: [How much does Square Share cost?](/en/pricing)

## Where can I check this in the dashboard?

The **Plans** page at [dashboard.squareshare.eu/plans](https://dashboard.squareshare.eu/plans) has a calculator, "Which plan costs you least?": drag it to your sales in a month and it shows each plan's price plus its share of those sales. Once checkout is open and you have sales, the dashboard will also work out what a bigger plan would save you, based on your last 30 days.
