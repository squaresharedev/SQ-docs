<AnswerBlock>

This section will collect fixes for common problems. If your storefront or purchase button does not work, start with the check section on the matching install page. Each one lists the most common causes. If that does not answer it, email office@squareshare.eu and describe what happens.

</AnswerBlock>

## Where do I start?

Two pages cover the most common problems step by step:

- [Why is my storefront not showing on my website?](/en/troubleshooting/storefront-not-showing) walks through allowed domains, https, editor previews, changed snippets and the browser console message.
- [Why is my product page not live?](/en/troubleshooting/product-page-not-live) explains each message on the Overview page and how to fix it.
- [Why did my CSV import skip rows?](/en/troubleshooting/csv-import-problems) lists every message the product import shows, and how to fix the file.
- [What does this dashboard error message mean?](/en/troubleshooting/dashboard-error-messages) covers purchase links, images, download files, PDFs, allowed domains and seller details.

Each install page also ends with a "How do I check it worked?" section that lists the most common causes:

- [How do I add a storefront to my website?](/en/install/add-storefront-to-website#how-do-i-check-it-worked) covers why a storefront may not show: allowed domains, `https`, and the published version of your page.
- [How do I add a purchase button to my website?](/en/install/add-purchase-button#why-cant-i-copy-the-link) covers why the link may be locked.

## How do I contact Square Share?

Email office@squareshare.eu. Say what happens, on which address, and which browser or site builder you use.
