<AnswerBlock>

A product page opens for buyers only when four things are true: your seller details are complete, the product is Active, it sits on a storefront's grid, and that storefront has Show a product page turned on. Until then, buyers who follow the link see "This product isn't available". The Overview page tells you which part is missing.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I find out what is missing?

Open **Overview** in the dashboard. In the **Get set up** card, the line under **Publish its page** names the first thing that stops your page. Where a step can be fixed on another page, the row has a button that takes you there.

| The dashboard says | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Finish your seller details, then your page can go live." | Your trader name, address or contact email is missing | Fill in [your seller details](/en/seller-details) |
| "Once a product is on a storefront, its page can go live." | No storefront has this product on its grid | Open the designer and use **Add product** in the toolbar |
| "Set that product to Active so its page goes live." | The product is a Draft | Click **Edit product** and set it to **Active** |
| "Product pages are off on that storefront. Turn them on in the designer." | **Show a product page** is off | Click **Open designer** and turn it on in the **Product page** settings |
| "Your page goes live once the automatic check approves the product." | The product is waiting for Square Share's automatic content check | Nothing. The page goes live on its own once approved |
| "The automatic check paused that product. Open it to see what to change." | The automatic check found something to change | Click **Edit product** and read why |

When everything is in place the step reads "Your product page is live."

## What does a buyer see when the page is not live?

A page that says "This product isn't available", with the explanation "It may have been removed, or the link you followed is out of date." Square Share shows the same page whatever the reason, so a buyer never learns why.

## Why did a page that worked stop working?

A live page goes offline as soon as one of the four conditions stops being true. Look for a recent change:

- You set the product back to **Draft**.
- You removed the product from the storefront's grid, or deleted the storefront.
- You turned off **Show a product page** on that storefront.
- You cleared a required field in your seller details.

Remember to click **Save** in the designer. A change you have not saved does not reach buyers, and neither does the fix.

## Why does my purchase button link not work after I moved the product?

A product page address names its storefront as well as the product:

```text title="Product page address"
https://dashboard.squareshare.eu/s/STOREFRONT_ID/p/PRODUCT_ID
```

The page only opens while the product is on that storefront. If you take the product off one storefront and put it on another, links that name the first storefront stop working. Copy the link again from **Products**, the product's **...** menu, **Add to your website**, and replace it on your site.

## How do I check a page myself?

When the setup card shows "You're set up", use **Copy product page link** and open the link in a private browser window. That shows the page the way a buyer sees it. For a purchase button, **Try your button** in the **Add to your website** dialog does the same.
