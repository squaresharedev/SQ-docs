These docs cover adding Square Share to the website you already own. You will find step-by-step install instructions for the storefront embed and the purchase button, a guide to how payments work, troubleshooting fixes, and a glossary of every term used across the site.

</AnswerBlock>

## What do you want to add?

<SectionCards>

- [Add a storefront to your website](/en/install/add-storefront-to-website)
  Paste one snippet and a grid of your products shows on your page.
- [Add a purchase button](/en/install/add-purchase-button)
  Point a button on your site at one product's page.
- [Install with an AI coding tool](/en/install/install-with-ai-coding-tool)
  Give Claude Code, Cursor or Lovable a ready prompt and let it paste the code.

</SectionCards>

## Where do I start?

If you are new to Square Share, read [What is Square Share?](/en/what-is-square-share) first, then [How do I set up Square Share?](/en/set-up-square-share). Then follow the install guide for what you want to add: the [storefront embed](/en/install/add-storefront-to-website) or the [purchase button](/en/install/add-purchase-button).
