<AnswerBlock>

The Notifications bell at the top of the dashboard collects what happens in your account: team, orders, payments, stock, billing, security, policy and reviews. Email is not switched on for Square Share yet, so for now everything arrives in the dashboard only. In Settings you can already pick which emails you want later.

## What do notifications cover?

| Category | For example |
| --- | --- |
| Team | Someone was invited, or joined |
| Order | A new order to ship, a new download order, a withdrawn order |
| Payment | A payment dispute |
| Stock | A product is running low |
| Billing | Your plan changed, or a payment for it failed |
| Security | Your password or two-factor settings changed, a sign-in was approved or denied, an AI assistant was connected |
| Policy | A product or storefront was paused by an automatic check |
| Review | A new review, or one held back |
| System | Notices about Square Share itself |

Orders, payment disputes and reviews come from Square Share checkout, which is not open yet, so you will not see those for now.

## How do I read and clear them?

Click the bell in the dashboard's top bar. The bell shows how many are unread. On the notifications page you can:

- Show **All** or only **Unread**.
- Filter by **Category**.
- Click **Mark all read**.

## Which emails can I choose?

In **Settings**, the **Email notifications** card has four choices:

| Choice | What it sends |
| --- | --- |
| Sales | An email when something sells, with what to pack and where to send it |
| Reviews | An email when a buyer reviews one of your products |
| Square Share news and features | New dashboard features and improvements |
| Tips & marketplace news | Occasional ideas to help you sell |

Security and payment emails cannot be turned off. While email is off, the card says so: "Email isn't switched on for Square Share yet, so nothing below is sent."

## What's coming next?

Email notifications will start once email is switched on for Square Share. Your choices in the **Email notifications** card will apply from then. There is no date yet.
