<AnswerBlock>

Go to Settings, then Security, and click Set up two-factor authentication. Choose a passkey (Face ID, a fingerprint or your phone's screen lock) or an authenticator app that shows six-digit codes. Then save the ten recovery codes the dashboard gives you. From then on, signing in needs your password and that second step.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I turn it on?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Settings** and choose **Security**.
2. Click **Set up two-factor authentication**. The dashboard asks for your Square Share password to confirm it is you. If your account signs in with Google, you confirm with Google instead.
3. Choose **Passkey** or **Authenticator app**.
4. For a passkey, click **Create passkey** and follow your browser's prompt. For an app, scan the QR code with it and type the six-digit code it shows, then click **Verify and turn on**.
5. Save your recovery codes: copy them, or click **Download**. Tick "I've saved my recovery codes somewhere safe."

</Steps>

The dashboard confirms with "Two-factor authentication is on." Any other devices signed in to your account are signed out.

## Passkey or authenticator app?

The dashboard marks the passkey as **Recommended**. Either one works, and you can add both.

| | Passkey | Authenticator app |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you use | Face ID, a fingerprint or your phone's screen lock | A six-digit code from an app such as Google Authenticator |
| Anything to install | No | The app |
| Signing in | Your face, fingerprint or screen lock | Open the app and type the code |
| If setup gets stuck | If your browser cannot create passkeys, use an app instead | If the QR code will not scan, type the setup key |

To keep a passkey on your phone while you set it up on a computer, choose your phone when the browser asks, then scan the QR code it shows with the phone's camera.

To add a second passkey or app later, use **Add another passkey or app** in the same card. A second one means losing a single device does not lock you out.

## What are recovery codes for?

They are your way back in if you lose your phone. You get ten. Each works once.

- Keep them somewhere other than your phone, such as a password manager.
- You only see them when they are made. If you lose them, use **Generate new codes** under **Recovery codes**. Your old codes stop working as soon as the new ones are made.
- The **Recovery codes** card shows how many you have left.

## How do I sign in with a recovery code?

On the sign-in screen, choose **Use a recovery code instead** and type one of your codes.

Using a code turns two-factor authentication off and signs out your other devices, so you can set it up again on a new phone. Do that straight away: until you do, your password is the only lock on the account.

If you have lost your device and every code, the sign-in screen says to write to support@squareshare.eu for help getting back in.

## Does it change what an AI assistant can do?

Yes, a little. When you connect an assistant to your account, the dashboard asks for your second step before it lets the assistant in, unless you passed it in the last ten minutes. See [How do I connect an AI assistant to Square Share?](/en/account/connect-ai-assistant).
