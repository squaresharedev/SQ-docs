<AnswerBlock>

Square Share is storefront infrastructure for people who already have a website. You add a product grid or a purchase button to your site by pasting a snippet of code. Buyers open a product page that Square Share hosts. They pay you directly, and Square Share never holds your money.

## What can I do with Square Share today?

**Storefront embed.** Show a grid of your products on any page of your own website. You design the storefront in the dashboard and paste one snippet of code into your page.

**Purchase button.** Put a link on a button of your own website. When a buyer clicks it, they land on the product page Square Share hosts for that product.

**Storefront editor (grid mode).** Design how your storefront looks before you publish it. The editor gives you:

- Three preset looks (Minimal, Classic, Bold), each of which sets colours, font, corners, spacing, and tile content in one step.
- Font upload (WOFF2, WOFF, TTF, or OTF up to 2 MB).
- Colour controls for background, accent, text, shape fill, and border.
- Block rotation (-180 to 180 degrees).
- Text alignment: left, center, or right.
- Block size adjusted by dragging edges.
- Grid density (0 to 32 px between cells).
- Corner roundness for cards, price tags, and shapes.
- Border thickness for price tags and shapes.
- Show or hide the product title on hover.
- Price tag position: Below, On image, or Hidden, with seven placement spots on the image.

## Who is Square Share for?

Square Share is for people who already own a website and want to sell from it, without moving to a separate platform. You keep your existing site and add selling to it.

To sign up you must be 18 or over and based in the EU, the USA, or Canada.

## How does Square Share work?

1. Create an account at [dashboard.squareshare.eu](https://dashboard.squareshare.eu). Complete your seller details and add at least one product.
2. Open the storefront editor in the dashboard, design your storefront, and copy the embed snippet from the Share page.
3. Paste the snippet into your website. Your storefront is live on your published page.

For the complete install procedure, see [How do I add a storefront to my website?](/en/install/add-storefront-to-website).

## How do payments and payouts work?

Buyers pay you directly. You are the [merchant of record](/en/reference/glossary#merchant-of-record) for every sale, and Square Share never holds your funds.

## Is Square Share the same as Square?

No. Square Share is operated by Root Labs and is not affiliated with Square (Block, Inc.) in any way.

## What's coming next?

The team plans to add a freeform editor mode for free placement of blocks, templates, and hosted storefronts on app.squareshare.eu for sellers who do not have their own site. A discovery feed or marketplace, more widgets (email signup, reservations, booking, and an availability calendar), and plugins for WordPress and Wix are also planned.

Dates are not set. Features listed here are not yet available.
