<AnswerBlock>

Create an account at dashboard.squareshare.eu, then follow the Get set up card on the Overview page. It has four steps: add your seller details, add a product, put the product on a storefront, and publish its page. When all four are done, your first product page is live and you can share its link.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I create an account?

<Steps>

1. Open [dashboard.squareshare.eu/login?mode=signup](https://dashboard.squareshare.eu/login?mode=signup).
2. Sign up with an email address and a password, or choose **Continue with Google**. You can also choose **Email me a sign-in link instead** and sign in from your inbox.
3. Click **Create account**. With an email and password, the dashboard asks you to confirm the address from a link in your inbox.

</Steps>

By creating an account you confirm that you are 18 or over and based in the EU, the USA or Canada. Square Share does not operate anywhere else yet.

## What are the four setup steps?

The Overview page shows a card called **Get set up**, with a line that runs through each step and a count such as "0 of 4 done". On a new account it lists these steps:

<SetupMap>

1. **Add your seller details**
   Your trader name, address and contact email go on every product page. [How to fill them in](/en/seller-details)
2. **Add a product**
   A title and a price are enough. [How to add a product](/en/products-and-storefronts/add-a-product)
3. **Put a product on a storefront**
   Create a storefront, then add a product to its grid. [How to design a storefront](/en/products-and-storefronts/design-your-storefront)
4. **Publish its page**
   Finish your seller details, then your page can go live. [When is a page live?](#when-is-a-product-page-live)

</SetupMap>

Each step you can take now has a button that takes you to the right place in the dashboard. A step that waits on an earlier one has none. The steps are worked out from what is in your account each time you open the page, so a step comes back if you undo it (for example, if you set your only product back to Draft).

## When is a product page live?

A product page opens for buyers only when all of these are true:

| Condition | Where you set it |
| --- | --- |
| Your trader name, address and contact email are filled in | Settings, **Business & seller details** |
| The product's status is **Active**, not Draft | The product's edit page |
| The product sits on a storefront's grid | The storefront designer, **Add product** in the toolbar |
| That storefront has **Show a product page** turned on | The storefront designer, **Product page** settings |

If the step stays open, the line under **Publish its page** says which condition is missing. [Why is my product page not live?](/en/troubleshooting/product-page-not-live) lists every message and its fix.

## What do I do when setup is finished?

The card changes to "You're set up" and offers **Copy product page link**. From there, choose how buyers reach your products:

<Buttons>

[Add a storefront to your website](/en/install/add-storefront-to-website) [Add a purchase button](/en/install/add-purchase-button)

</Buttons>
