<AnswerBlock>

Go to Settings, then Business & seller details, and fill in your trader name, address and contact email. These three are required: until they are complete, no product page goes live and nothing can be sold. Buyers see them in the Seller section of every product page, so they know who they are buying from.

## How do I add my seller details?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Settings**.
2. Choose **Business & seller details**.
3. Fill in **Trader name**, **Address** and **Contact email**.
4. Add any of the optional fields you want buyers to see: **Country**, **VAT ID** and **Bio**.
5. Save. The dashboard confirms with "Business & seller details saved."

</Steps>

You set these once for your whole account. They apply to every product page you sell on.

## Which fields are required, and who sees them?

| Field | Required | What buyers see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trader name | Yes | Your name at the top of the Seller section |
| Address | Yes | Your postal address, as you typed it |
| Contact email | Yes | Your address as a link they can email |
| Country | No | The country name, if you choose one |
| VAT ID | No | Your VAT ID, if you add one |
| Bio | No | One line under your name |
| Phone | No | Nothing today (see below) |

## What name should I use?

Use the name buyers are contracting with. If you sell through a registered business, that is the business's registered name. If you sell as an individual, it is your own full name.

Your storefront's name does not count. A storefront name is a shop name, and the dashboard does not accept it in place of the trader name.

## Which contact email should I use?

Use an address you read. It does not have to be the one you sign in with, and the dashboard keeps the two separate on purpose.

The dashboard refuses addresses nobody reads: placeholders, temporary inbox services, no-reply addresses, and domains that take no mail.

Your contact email has a second job. When a product has no purchase link of its own, the button on its product page becomes **Ask about this product** and opens an email to this address.

## Why does the phone number not show?

Buyers only see a phone number once it is confirmed with a code sent by text message. Confirming by text is not available yet, so a phone number you add stays hidden from buyers for now.

## What does Square Share do with these details?

They appear on your product pages so buyers can see who they are buying from and how to reach you. The dashboard states that Square Share never uses them for marketing and never sells or shares them. Details are in the [Square Share privacy policy](https://squareshare.eu/legal/privacy-policy/).

## What changes once they are complete?

The dashboard stops blocking you. Until the three required fields are filled in, it shows messages such as "You can't publish or sell anything until your seller details are complete." on your products, storefronts and the Share page.

The first step of the **Get set up** card on the Overview page is ticked off. See [How do I set up Square Share?](/en/set-up-square-share) for the remaining steps.
