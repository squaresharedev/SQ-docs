<AnswerBlock>

When you sell as a business, EU consumer law asks you to tell buyers who you are and how to reach you before they buy: your identity, your geographical address, and your phone number and email where you have them. Square Share shows your trader name, address and contact email on every product page, and publishes nothing without them.

This page explains what the rules list and how Square Share covers them. It is not legal advice.

## What do the EU rules list?

| Rule | What it asks you to give buyers |
| --- | --- |
| [Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2011/83/oj), Article 6(1)(b) and (c) | Your identity, such as your trading name. The geographical address where you are established. Your telephone number and email address, where available. |
| [E-Commerce Directive 2000/31/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2000/31/oj), Article 5 | Your name, geographic address and email. Your trade register and registration number, if you are registered. Your VAT identification number, if your activity is subject to VAT. |

## How does Square Share cover each item?

| What the rules ask for | Square Share field | On the product page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identity | **Trader name** (required) | Yes, at the top of the Seller section |
| Geographical address | **Address** (required) | Yes |
| Email | **Contact email** (required) | Yes, as a link |
| Telephone number | **Phone** (optional) | Not yet: it needs confirming by text message, which is not available |
| VAT number | **VAT ID** (optional) | Yes, when you add one |
| Trade register number | No field of its own | No |

You fill these in once, in **Settings** under **Business & seller details**. See [How do I fill in my seller details?](/en/seller-details)

Each product page also says who the seller is in its footer: you are "responsible for the product, its delivery, and any returns or refunds", and Square Share "is not a party to the sale."

## Does this apply if I sell as a private person?

The Consumer Rights Directive applies to traders: people and companies acting "for purposes relating to his trade, business, craft or profession". Square Share asks every seller for the same three details before anything publishes, whether or not the rules apply to them.

## Do I need a phone number?

The directive asks for a phone number "where available". The EU Court of Justice has said this does not oblige traders to set up a phone line only for this purpose (case C-649/17). In Square Share, a phone number you add stays hidden from buyers until confirming by text is available.

## Where can I read more?

The EU's official [Your Europe page on selling online](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm) summarises what online sellers must tell consumers. Your country's consumer protection authority can say how the rules apply where you sell.
