Add a product, choose A download, and upload the file: ZIP, PDF, EPUB, MP3, WAV, MP4, JPEG, PNG, WebP or TXT, up to 200 MB. Square Share does not deliver files yet. Until its checkout opens, buyers pay through your own purchase link or email you, and the file reaches them from that checkout or from you.

## How do I add a download product?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products** and click **Add product**.
2. Enter the **Title** and price.
3. Under **What are you selling?**, choose **A download**.
4. Upload the file.
5. Under **Visibility**, choose **Active**, then click **Save product**.
6. Put the product on a storefront with product pages turned on, so it gets a page.

</Steps>

The dashboard notes that a download needs no shipping or product safety details, and the form hides its **Safety and compliance** section.

## Which files can I upload?

| Kind | Formats |
| --- | --- |
| Archives | ZIP |
| Documents | PDF, EPUB, TXT |
| Audio | MP3, WAV |
| Video | MP4 |
| Images | JPEG, PNG, WebP |

One file per product, up to 200 MB. Square Share checks that the file's contents match its type. A renamed file is refused with "That file's contents don't match its type." To sell several files together, put them in one ZIP.

## How does the buyer get the file today?

| How you sell | What the buyer clicks | How the file reaches them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| With a purchase link on the product | "Checkout on" and your checkout's name | From your own checkout. Point the link at a checkout that delivers files, or send the file yourself after payment. |
| Without a purchase link | **Ask about this product** | From you. The buyer's email reaches your contact email, you agree payment, and you send the file. |

Add a purchase link in the product form, under **Selling through your own checkout? Add its link**. It must start with `https://`.

## What does the product page show?

The page labels the product as a digital download, and names the file format where it can. Your seller details appear as on every product page.
