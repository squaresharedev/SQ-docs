<AnswerBlock>

Copy your storefront link from its Share page, or a product's link, and put it in your Instagram bio, on your link-in-bio page, or on a link sticker in a Story. Buyers who tap it open your storefront or product page on Square Share, so you need no website. Code snippets do not work on Instagram itself.

</AnswerBlock>

## Which link should I share?

| Link | Where to copy it | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storefront link | **Storefront**, then **Share** on your storefront, under **Storefront link**: **Copy storefront link** | The whole storefront on a page of its own |
| Product link | **Products**, the **...** menu on a product, **Add to your website**, the **Link** tab | One product's page |

The storefront link appears once product pages are turned on in the designer and your seller details are complete. The dashboard describes it this way: "Your storefront has a page of its own. Anyone with this link can browse it, no website needed."

## Where do I put the link on Instagram?

- **Your bio.** In the Instagram app, open your profile, tap **Edit profile**, then **Links**, then **Add external link**, and paste the link. Instagram lets you add several links, and shows the first one under your bio.
- **A Story.** Add the **Link** sticker to a Story and paste the link. Each Story takes one link sticker.
- **A link-in-bio page.** If you already use a page that collects your links, such as Linktree, add your storefront or product link to it as one more button.

## What happens when a follower taps it?

Instagram opens your Square Share page in its own browser. From a product page, the buyer pays through the purchase link you set on the product, or emails you with **Ask about this product**. See [Can I sell from my website without an online shop?](/en/selling/sell-without-online-shop)

## Can I put the storefront itself on Instagram?

No. Instagram does not run pasted code, so there is nothing to embed: share a link instead. If you also have a website, the storefront can sit on it too. See [Which site builders does Square Share work with?](/en/site-builders)
