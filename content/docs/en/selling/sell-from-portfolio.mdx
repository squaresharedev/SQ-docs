<AnswerBlock>

Add each piece you sell as a product in the dashboard. Then put a storefront on a page of your portfolio, or add a purchase button under each piece. Prints and objects are products you ship, and files are downloads. Your portfolio stays on the site builder you already use.

</AnswerBlock>

## Should I use a storefront or purchase buttons?

| Option | Fits when | What the visitor sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storefront | You want one page that lists everything for sale | A grid of your pieces. A click opens the piece's product page. |
| Purchase button | Each project page already shows one piece | A button under the piece that opens its product page |

You can use both: a shop page with the storefront, and buttons on the project pages.

## How do I set up each piece?

Add it as a product: a title and a price are enough to save. These parts of the product form fit artwork and editions:

| Part of the form | Use it for | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos | Details, framing, the piece on a wall | 24 extra photos, each up to 10 MB |
| Options | Print sizes or paper types | 4 groups, 48 options in total |
| Stock | A limited edition: a count that goes down to zero | Up to 100 per order |
| Badges | Short labels such as "Handmade" | 6 badges, 3 of them on the photo |
| Specifications | Dimensions, materials, care | 30 rows |

Original files, presets or templates are [digital downloads](/en/selling/sell-digital-downloads) instead.

Full form details: [How do I add a product?](/en/products-and-storefronts/add-a-product)

## Will I always need my own website?

Not forever. We plan to offer hosted portfolios on app.squareshare.eu. You will be able to build your following on Square Share without a website of your own, and start selling when you are ready, to an audience that is already there. This is planned: it is not available yet and there is no date. Until then, your portfolio stays on the site builder you use.

## How do buyers pay?

Through the purchase link you add to the product, which opens your own checkout, or by emailing you from the product page. Square Share checkout will take card payments on the page once it opens. See [Can I sell from my website without an online shop?](/en/selling/sell-without-online-shop)
