<AnswerBlock>

Add a product as A download, upload the file, and fill in your seller details. Add a purchase link to each product so buyers can pay through your own checkout. As an EU seller you are merchant of record, so VAT on digital sales to consumers in other EU countries is yours to declare.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I list a digital download?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products** and click **Add product**.
2. Enter a title and price.
3. Under **What are you selling?**, choose **A download** and upload the file.
4. Set the product to **Active** and click **Save product**.
5. Put the product on a storefront with product pages turned on.

</Steps>

Accepted formats: ZIP, PDF, EPUB, MP3, WAV, MP4, JPEG, PNG, WebP, TXT, up to 200 MB. See [How do I sell digital downloads from my website?](/en/selling/sell-digital-downloads) for the full details.

## How do buyers pay today?

Add a purchase link to each product under **Selling through your own checkout? Add its link**. The product page button opens that link in a new tab. A Stripe Payment Link, a Gumroad link, or any other `https://` checkout works.

If you leave the field empty, the button reads **Ask about this product** and opens an email to your contact address.

| What you set | The button | How the buyer pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A purchase link | "Checkout on" and the site's name | Through your own checkout |
| No purchase link | **Ask about this product** | By arrangement with you |

## What VAT applies to digital sales in the EU?

When you sell digital products to consumers in other EU countries, the buyer's country's VAT applies once your cross-border sales pass the EU-wide threshold. Below the threshold, your own country's rules apply. If you are based outside the EU, there is no threshold: the buyer's country's VAT applies from the first sale to an EU consumer.

The One Stop Shop (OSS) lets you register once in your own country and declare VAT for all other EU countries there, instead of registering in each one separately. Sellers based outside the EU use its non-Union scheme, registering in one EU country of their choice.

For the threshold amounts and the full rules, see [What do I need to know about VAT as an EU seller?](/en/payments-and-payouts/vat-and-taxes)

## What seller information must I show buyers?

EU law requires you to tell buyers your identity, your geographical address, and your phone number and email where available. SquareShare shows your trader name, address and contact email on every product page, and publishes nothing without them.

Fill these in once under **Settings**, then **Business and seller details**. See [What seller details does EU law ask me to show buyers?](/en/eu-seller-information)
