<AnswerBlock>

Partly, today. SquareShare puts your storefront and product pages on your own website, while Gumroad handles checkout, VAT and file delivery. You can keep Gumroad for checkout and link to it from each SquareShare product. Moving checkout away from Gumroad makes VAT and delivery yours to handle.

</AnswerBlock>

## What does each handle today?

| | SquareShare today | Gumroad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storefront on your own site | Yes, a grid of your products from a snippet you paste in | Gumroad's own embed and overlay buttons |
| Hosted product page | Yes | Yes |
| Merchant of record | You | Gumroad (for checkout sales) |
| VAT on digital sales | Yours to handle. See [VAT and taxes](/en/payments-and-payouts/vat-and-taxes) | Gumroad collects it for sales through its checkout |
| File delivery | Manual today (SquareShare checkout not yet open) | Automatic after payment |
| Checkout | Your own purchase link (Gumroad, Stripe, or any other `https://` URL) | Gumroad's own |

## Can I keep Gumroad for checkout?

Yes. You can run SquareShare and Gumroad together: add each product to SquareShare and paste its Gumroad link as the product's purchase link. Buyers land on your SquareShare product page, then pay through Gumroad. Gumroad keeps handling payment, file delivery and VAT for those sales.

This approach lets you try the SquareShare storefront without changing how buyers pay.

## What changes if I move fully to SquareShare?

When you stop using Gumroad's checkout, you become the seller for the whole transaction. That means:

- **VAT on digital sales to EU consumers:** yours to register and declare. See [What do I need to know about VAT as an EU seller?](/en/payments-and-payouts/vat-and-taxes)
- **File delivery:** today you send it yourself. SquareShare checkout will deliver it after payment once it opens.
- **Payment:** through your own purchase link, such as a Stripe Payment Link, until SquareShare checkout opens.
- **Seller information:** your trader name, address and contact email must appear on every product page. See [What seller details does EU law ask me to show buyers?](/en/eu-seller-information)

For a step-by-step migration, see [How do I switch from Gumroad to SquareShare?](/en/selling/switch-from-gumroad)

## What's coming next?

SquareShare checkout will take card payments on the product page, into your own Stripe account, and will deliver files after payment. It is not open yet, and there is no date.
