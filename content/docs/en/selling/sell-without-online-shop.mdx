<AnswerBlock>

Yes. Square Share adds selling to the website you already have. You paste a storefront snippet or a purchase button into your site, and each product gets a page that Square Share hosts. Until Square Share checkout opens, buyers pay through your own purchase link or contact you by email.

</AnswerBlock>

## What do I get instead of an online shop?

| Part | What it does | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Storefront | A grid of your products that you design | On a page of your website, through one snippet |
| Purchase button | A link that opens one product's page | On any button or link of your website |
| Product page | Photos, price, details, your seller details, and the button to pay or ask | Hosted by Square Share |
| Dashboard | Products, storefronts, discounts and analytics | Online, where you sign in |

Your website stays on the host or site builder you use now. See [Which site builders does Square Share work with?](/en/site-builders)

## How do buyers pay today?

What the product page's button does depends on one setting in each product:

| What you set | The button | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A purchase link on the product | "Checkout on" and the site's name | Your own checkout, in a new tab |
| No purchase link | **Ask about this product** | An email to your contact email, with the product's name in the subject |

Square Share checkout will take card payments on the product page, into your own Stripe account. It is not open yet.

## What stays my job?

You are the seller. Every product page says so in its footer: you are "responsible for the product, its delivery, and any returns or refunds", and Square Share "provides the technology behind this page and is not a party to the sale."

That means you:

- Take payment, through your own checkout or by arrangement with the buyer.
- Send the product, or the file for a download.
- Answer buyers' emails and handle returns.
- Keep your [seller details](/en/seller-details) complete. Nothing publishes without them.

## How do I start?

<Steps>

1. Create an account at [dashboard.squareshare.eu](https://dashboard.squareshare.eu). You must be 18 or over and based in the EU, the USA or Canada.
2. Fill in your seller details and add a product.
3. Put the product on a storefront and publish its page.
4. Paste the storefront or a purchase button into your website.

</Steps>

The **Get set up** card on the dashboard's Overview page walks you through the first three steps. See [How do I set up Square Share?](/en/set-up-square-share)
