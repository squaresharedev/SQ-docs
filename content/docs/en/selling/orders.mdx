<AnswerBlock>

Orders in the dashboard lists sales made through Square Share checkout. That checkout is not open yet, so the list stays empty for now. Sales through your own purchase link or by email do not show there: track them in your own checkout tool or inbox, where the payment or the buyer's message arrives.

</AnswerBlock>

## Why is my Orders list empty?

The page says so itself: "No orders yet", with the line "Square Share checkout isn't open yet. Sales through your own buy link or by email won't show up here."

| How the buyer paid | Shows in Orders? | Where to look instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Through your own purchase link | No | The checkout tool the link opens |
| By email, after **Ask about this product** | No | Your inbox, at your contact email |
| Through Square Share checkout | Yes, once it opens | |

There is no way to add an order by hand.

## Can I see how many people looked at a product?

Yes. **Analytics** counts storefront views, product clicks and product page views today, whichever way buyers pay. See [What does Square Share analytics measure?](/en/selling/analytics)

## What's coming next?

When Square Share checkout opens, each sale will appear in **Orders**. None of this works yet, and there is no date.

- A **To ship** view with paid orders that still need sending, oldest first, next to **All orders**.
- Columns for product, amount, channel, status, buyer, date and shipping, with search and filters.
- Each order will show what to pack, the delivery address and any gift message.
- **Mark as shipped**, with an optional tracking number and carrier.
- **Refund or cancel**, for the account owner.
- Exporting orders to a CSV file, depending on your plan.
