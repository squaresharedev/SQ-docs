<AnswerBlock>

You can move step by step. Add each product to Square Share and, if you want Gumroad to keep handling payment and delivery, paste its Gumroad link as the product's purchase link. Your storefront then sits on your own website. Moving fully means you deliver files and handle tax yourself, because you become the seller.

</AnswerBlock>

## Can I keep Gumroad for checkout?

Yes. Square Share accepts any `https://` address as a product's purchase link, including a Gumroad product link (it looks like `https://gumroad.com/l/your-product`). The product page's button opens it in a new tab, and Gumroad takes the payment and sends the buyer the file, as it does today.

## How do I move a product over?

<Steps>

1. Add the product in Square Share under **Products**, **Add product**, with the same title and price.
2. For a file, choose **A download** and upload it, so the product is ready for Square Share checkout later.
3. Paste the Gumroad link under **Selling through your own checkout? Add its link**, or leave it empty to have buyers email you.
4. Set the product to **Active**, save it, and put it on a storefront with product pages turned on.

</Steps>

Gumroad's help centre describes CSV exports of sales and customers, not of products. Add products by hand, or build a CSV with **Title** and **Price** columns and use the [CSV import](/en/troubleshooting/csv-import-problems).

## What changes if I stop using Gumroad's checkout?

| | Gumroad checkout | Square Share without a Gumroad link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who is the seller | Gumroad says it acts as merchant of record and handles sales tax | You are the seller, under your own [seller details](/en/seller-details) |
| VAT and sales tax | Gumroad collects it where it has tax obligations | Yours to handle. See [VAT and taxes](/en/payments-and-payouts/vat-and-taxes) |
| File delivery | Gumroad emails the buyer a download link | Today: you send the file. Square Share checkout will deliver it once open |
| Payment | Gumroad pays you out | Today: your own checkout or by email. Later: your own Stripe account |
