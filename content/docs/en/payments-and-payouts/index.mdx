<AnswerBlock>

When a buyer completes a purchase, payment goes through your own Stripe account directly to you. You are the merchant of record for every sale. Square Share never holds your money. This section will grow as more payment features ship.

Payments run through Stripe, into your own Stripe account, under Stripe's terms. Square Share never holds your money.

Until Stripe checkout is available in the product, buyers reach you through your product's purchase link or by emailing you directly.
