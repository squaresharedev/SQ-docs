<AnswerBlock>

Buyers pay you directly, and you are the merchant of record for every sale. Square Share never holds your money. Square Share checkout will take payments into your own Stripe account. Until it is available, buyers pay through your product's purchase link or by email. This section will grow as payment features ship.

</AnswerBlock>

## Who is the seller of record?

You are. That is why the dashboard asks for your [seller details](/en/reference/glossary#seller-details) (name, address and contact email) before anything publishes or sells.
