Today buyers pay you through your own purchase link or by email, and the money goes straight to you: SquareShare never touches it and charges nothing. When SquareShare checkout opens, payments will go into your own Stripe account, and Stripe pays your balance out to your bank on a rolling schedule.

## What does SquareShare cost?

Nothing today: paid plans and SquareShare checkout are not switched on yet, and the dashboard says "Paid plans open soon. Everything on Free keeps working in the meantime." When they open, Free is €0, Starter €12 and Pro €39 a month before VAT, plus 5%, 2% or 0.5% of each sale made through SquareShare checkout. Sellers who join during early access get Pro free for six months.

Sales through your own purchase link or by email never pass through SquareShare, so no share is taken on them, on any plan.

Every plan, what each includes and worked examples: [How much does SquareShare cost?](/en/pricing)

## What does Stripe charge?

SquareShare checkout will run on your own Stripe account, so Stripe charges its own card processing fee on each sale, on top of SquareShare's share. Stripe's rates depend on your country and the buyer's card: see [Stripe's pricing page](https://stripe.com/pricing). In the dashboard, an order's "You receive" line is shown "Before Stripe's card fee".

## How will payouts reach my bank?

Payments will go into your own Stripe account, not to SquareShare: you are the [merchant of record](/en/reference/glossary#merchant-of-record), and SquareShare never holds your money. Stripe then sends your balance to your bank account automatically, on a rolling schedule that Stripe sets. The dashboard's **Payments** page will show what is available, what is still pending and the date of the next payout.

Until checkout opens, the Payments page says "Getting paid through Stripe is coming soon", and you are paid however your own checkout pays you.

## Who is the seller of record?

You are. That is why the dashboard asks for your [seller details](/en/reference/glossary#seller-details) (name, address and contact email) before anything publishes or sells. See [What seller details does EU law ask me to show buyers?](/en/eu-seller-information)
