<AnswerBlock>

Today you refund a buyer where they paid: in your own checkout tool for a purchase link, or directly for a sale arranged by email. Square Share never held the money, so there is nothing to refund in the dashboard. Once Square Share checkout opens, the account owner will refund an order with Refund or cancel in Orders.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I refund a buyer today?

| How the buyer paid | How to refund |
| --- | --- |
| Through your product's purchase link | In the checkout tool that link opens (Stripe, Gumroad, your own shop), with its refund function |
| By arrangement after **Ask about this product** | Pay the money back yourself, the same way you were paid |

You are the seller, so refunds, returns and the legal deadlines are yours. Product pages already tell buyers in the EU: "You can cancel this purchase within 14 days of receiving it without giving a reason, unless one of the legal exceptions applies (for example made-to-order or sealed goods)."

## What changes when Square Share checkout opens?

Each sale will appear in **Orders**, and the order will have a **Refund or cancel** action:

<Steps>

1. Open the order in **Orders** and click **Refund or cancel**.
2. Pick a reason: "Cancelled before sending", "Returned by the buyer" or "Something else".
3. Choose whether to put the items back in stock.
4. Confirm. The whole order is refunded to the buyer's card through Stripe, and this cannot be undone.

</Steps>

Only the store's owner will be able to refund or cancel an order. Refunds will cover the whole order.

## What will the buyer see?

The buyer will get an email saying the refund is on its way. For a card payment, the email says: "It goes back the way you paid, which usually takes 5 to 10 days."

If a buyer cancels within the 14 days through their order page, the order will show "Buyer cancelled", and the dashboard will ask you to "Arrange the return and issue the refund within 14 days."
