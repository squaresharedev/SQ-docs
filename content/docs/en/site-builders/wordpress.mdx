In the WordPress block editor, add a Custom HTML block, paste the snippet from your storefront's Share page and publish. On your own WordPress install, an Administrator or Editor account must save it. On WordPress.com, scripts need a paid plan with hosting features activated. For one product, link a Buttons block to its product page.

## What do I need first?

- A storefront in the dashboard with **Show on my website** turned on on its **Share** page.
- Your site's domain in **Allowed domains**, saved. On WordPress.com without your own domain, that is your `yoursite.wordpress.com` address.
- Permission to save scripts. See "Why did WordPress remove my snippet?" below.

## How do I add a storefront to a WordPress page?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Storefront**, click **Share** on your storefront, and choose **WordPress** under **Your site builder**.
2. Keep the **Script** tab and click **Copy embed snippet**.
3. In WordPress, edit the page or post. Click the block inserter (**+**), search for **Custom HTML**, and add the block where the storefront should go. You can also type `/html` on an empty line.
4. Paste the snippet into the block.
5. Click **Update** or **Publish**.

</Steps>

<Snippet>

```html title="Script snippet"
<div data-squareshare-storefront="YOUR-EMBED-KEY"></div>
<script async src="https://embed.squareshare.eu/widget.js"></script>
```

</Snippet>

The block's preview in the editor does not run scripts. Open the published page to see the storefront. It grows to fit, so you set no height.

Using the classic editor? Switch to the **Text** tab and paste the snippet there. Page builders work too, as long as you paste into an element meant for HTML, such as Elementor's HTML widget.

## Why did WordPress remove my snippet?

WordPress strips `<script>` and `<iframe>` tags from content saved by accounts that are not allowed to post unfiltered HTML.

| Where your site runs | Who can save the snippet |
| --- | --- |
| Your own WordPress install | Administrators and Editors. Authors and Contributors have scripts removed when they save. |
| WordPress Multisite | Only Super Admins. |
| WordPress.com | According to [WordPress.com's Custom HTML block guide](https://wordpress.com/support/wordpress-editor/blocks/custom-html-block/), "tags like iframe and script require a paid plan with hosting features activated". |

If you cannot save scripts, a purchase button still works: it is a plain link, and WordPress keeps links.

## How do I add a purchase button on WordPress?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products**, click the **...** menu on the product, and choose **Add to your website**.
2. On the **Link** tab, copy the link.
3. In WordPress, add a **Buttons** block and type the button's text.
4. Click the link icon in the block toolbar, paste the link and press Enter.
5. Click **Update** or **Publish**.

</Steps>

Full details: [How do I add a purchase button to my website?](/en/install/add-purchase-button)

## Why is the storefront not showing on WordPress?

- **The script was stripped.** Edit the block again. If the `<script>` line is gone, see the table above.
- **You are looking at the editor.** Check the published page.
- **The address is not listed.** The domain in the browser bar must be in **Allowed domains**.
- **A caching or optimisation plugin changed the script.** Exclude `embed.squareshare.eu/widget.js` from script combining or delaying, or use the **Iframe only** version.

The full checklist is on [Why is my storefront not showing on my website?](/en/troubleshooting/storefront-not-showing)
