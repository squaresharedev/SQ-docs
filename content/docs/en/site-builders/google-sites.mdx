<AnswerBlock>

In Google Sites, choose Insert, then Embed, then Embed code, paste the snippet from your storefront's Share page and publish. Google Sites runs pasted code in a frame of its own, which Square Share recognises, so you only list your site's address in Allowed domains. For one product, link a button to its product page.

</AnswerBlock>

## What do I need first?

- A storefront in the dashboard with **Show on my website** turned on on its **Share** page.
- Your site's address in **Allowed domains**, saved. A site on a free Google account lives at `sites.google.com/view/yoursite`: list `sites.google.com`. With your own domain, list that domain.

## How do I add a storefront to a Google Sites page?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Storefront**, click **Share** on your storefront, and choose **Google Sites** under **Your site builder**.
2. Keep the **Script** tab and click **Copy embed snippet**.
3. In Google Sites, open the page, choose **Insert** in the panel on the right, then **Embed**.
4. Choose **Embed code**, paste the snippet and click **Next**, then **Insert**.
5. Drag the box as tall as your storefront.
6. Click **Publish**.

</Steps>

<Snippet>

```html title="Script snippet"
<div data-squareshare-storefront="YOUR-EMBED-KEY"></div>
<script async src="https://embed.squareshare.eu/widget.js"></script>
```

</Snippet>

## Why does it run in a frame?

Google Sites never puts pasted code on the page itself: it serves it from a Google address of its own, inside a frame. Square Share knows that frame, so the storefront shows as long as your site's own address is in **Allowed domains**. The frame keeps the size you give it, so make the box tall enough for every row of products.

## How do I add a purchase button on Google Sites?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products**, click the **...** menu on the product, and choose **Add to your website**.
2. On the **Link** tab, copy the link.
3. In Google Sites, choose **Insert**, then **Button**.
4. Give the button a name, paste the link and click **Insert**. Then publish.

</Steps>

Full details: [How do I add a purchase button to my website?](/en/install/add-purchase-button)

## Why is the storefront not showing on Google Sites?

- **You are looking at the editor.** Check the published site.
- **The address is not listed.** List `sites.google.com` for a site on a free Google account, or your own domain.
- **The box is too small.** Drag it taller.

The full checklist is on [Why is my storefront not showing on my website?](/en/troubleshooting/storefront-not-showing)
