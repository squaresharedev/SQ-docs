<AnswerBlock>

A public Notion page cannot run pasted code, so link to Square Share instead: a product's link, or your storefront's own page. Notion-based site builders are different: Super, for example, runs custom code on its paid plans, so the storefront snippet can go there. Links work on every plan of both.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I link to a product from Notion?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products**, click the **...** menu on the product, and choose **Add to your website**.
2. On the **Link** tab, copy the link.
3. In Notion, select the text you want buyers to click (for example "Buy the print"), and paste the link onto it.

</Steps>

A Notion embed block shows a web address inside a frame. Product pages refuse to load in frames, so use a plain link rather than an embed.

## How do I link to my whole storefront?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Storefront** and click **Share** on your storefront.
2. Under **Storefront link**, click **Copy storefront link**.
3. Paste it as a link on your Notion page.

</Steps>

The storefront link appears once product pages are turned on in the designer and your seller details are complete.

## Can I embed the storefront on a Super site?

Yes, on a Super plan that includes custom code (Super's pricing page says the free plan does not). Super's guide works like this:

<Steps>

1. In the Notion page behind your Super site, type `/code` to add a code block.
2. On its first line, type `super-embed:`.
3. Paste the snippet from your storefront's **Share** page below it (choose **Another site** under **Your site builder**).
4. Add your Super site's address to **Allowed domains**, and check the published site.

</Steps>

<Snippet>

```html title="Code block on a Super site"
super-embed:
<div data-squareshare-storefront="YOUR-EMBED-KEY"></div>
<script async src="https://embed.squareshare.eu/widget.js"></script>
```

</Snippet>
