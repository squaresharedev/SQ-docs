## What do I need first?

- A storefront in the dashboard with **Show on my website** turned on on its **Share** page.
- Your addresses in **Allowed domains**, saved. List your own domain, and your staging address `yoursite.webflow.io` if you want to see the storefront there too.
- A Webflow site plan that includes the Code Embed element. Webflow lists it as a paid plan feature in its [Code Embed lesson](https://university.webflow.com/course-lesson/custom-code-at-the-layout-level-code-embed-element).

## How do I add a storefront to a Webflow page?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Storefront**, click **Share** on your storefront, and choose **Webflow** under **Your site builder**.
2. Keep the **Script** tab and click **Copy embed snippet**.
3. In the Webflow Designer, open the Add panel and drag a **Code Embed** element to where the storefront should go.
4. Paste the snippet into the code editor that opens, then click **Save & Close**.
5. Publish the site.

</Steps>

<Snippet>

```html title="Script snippet"
<div data-squareshare-storefront="YOUR-EMBED-KEY"></div>
<script async src="https://embed.squareshare.eu/widget.js"></script>
```

</Snippet>

The Designer canvas shows a placeholder, not your storefront. Pasted code runs on the published site and in Webflow's preview of it.

## Why does it work on my domain but not on webflow.io?

Because `yoursite.webflow.io` is a different domain from `yoursite.com`. Square Share only draws the storefront on addresses listed in **Allowed domains**, and there are no wildcards. Add the `webflow.io` address if you check your work there before publishing to your own domain. Remove it later if you no longer use it.

## How big is the storefront?

The **Script** version grows to fit your storefront, so you do not set a height. Its width follows the element it sits in: put the Code Embed in a section or container as wide as you want the storefront.

## How do I add a purchase button on Webflow?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products**, click the **...** menu on the product, and choose **Add to your website**.
2. On the **Link** tab, copy the link.
3. In Webflow, select a **Button** element (or any link) and open its link settings.
4. Choose a URL link and paste the product link.
5. Publish the site.

</Steps>

The button needs no script, so it works on any Webflow plan. Full details: [How do I add a purchase button to my website?](/en/install/add-purchase-button)

## Why is the storefront not showing on Webflow?

- **You are looking at the Designer.** Check the published site.
- **The address is not listed.** Add the exact address from the browser bar to **Allowed domains**, including `webflow.io` if you use it.
- **The site plan does not include Code Embed.** Use the purchase button, or change plan.

The full checklist is on [Why is my storefront not showing on my website?](/en/troubleshooting/storefront-not-showing)
