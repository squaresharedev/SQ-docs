<AnswerBlock>

On GoDaddy Website Builder, use links: point a button at a product page, or at your storefront's own page. The storefront snippet does not show there, because GoDaddy runs pasted code inside a frame of its own, and Square Share only draws a storefront on the sites you list. Links need no code and work on any plan.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I add a purchase button on GoDaddy?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products**, click the **...** menu on the product, and choose **Add to your website**.
2. On the **Link** tab, copy the link.
3. In GoDaddy Website Builder, add or edit a button in any section, and paste the link as its destination.
4. Publish the site.

</Steps>

Full details: [How do I add a purchase button to my website?](/en/install/add-purchase-button)

## How do I link to my whole storefront?

Every storefront with product pages turned on has a page of its own, with no website needed:

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Storefront** and click **Share** on your storefront.
2. Under **Storefront link**, click **Copy storefront link**.
3. In GoDaddy, put that link on a button or in your site's menu, and publish.

</Steps>

The storefront link only appears once product pages are turned on in the designer and your seller details are complete.

## Why does the storefront snippet not show on GoDaddy?

GoDaddy's **HTML** section places custom code inside a frame on a GoDaddy address, not on your page. Square Share's storefront refuses to show on any page that is not in your **Allowed domains**, and it only recognises the frames that Wix and Google Sites use. See [How do allowed domains work?](/en/install/allowed-domains)
