These are short answers to the questions sellers ask most often about Square Share: what it is, what it costs, how buyers pay, which site builders work, how to sell on Instagram, and who handles VAT and refunds. Each answer links to the page with the full steps and details.

</AnswerBlock>

## What is Square Share?

Square Share adds selling to the website you already have: a storefront or a purchase button that you paste in, and a hosted page for each product. [What is Square Share?](/en/what-is-square-share)

## What does Square Share cost?

Nothing today: paid plans are not open yet, and Square Share takes no share of sales made through your own purchase link. [What does Square Share cost, and how do I get paid?](/en/payments-and-payouts)

## How do buyers pay me?

Through the purchase link you add to each product, which opens your own checkout, or by emailing you from the product page. Square Share checkout, into your own Stripe account, is not open yet. [Can I sell from my website without an online shop?](/en/selling/sell-without-online-shop)

## Does Square Share hold my money?

No. You are the seller of record, and payments go to you or, once checkout opens, to your own Stripe account. [Payments and payouts](/en/payments-and-payouts)

## Who can sign up?

Sellers who are 18 or over and based in the EU, the USA or Canada. [How do I set up Square Share?](/en/set-up-square-share)

## Which site builders does it work with?

Any builder that accepts pasted HTML, including WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, Framer, Carrd, Google Sites and Shopify. Where code cannot be pasted, a purchase button still works because it is a plain link. [Which site builders does Square Share work with?](/en/site-builders)

## Can I sell without a website?

Yes. Every storefront with product pages turned on has a page of its own that you can share, for example from your Instagram bio. [How do I sell on Instagram with Square Share?](/en/selling/sell-on-instagram)

## Why is my storefront not showing on my site?

Usually because the page's address is not in Allowed domains, the page is not on https, or you are looking at the builder's editor instead of the published site. [Why is my storefront not showing on my website?](/en/troubleshooting/storefront-not-showing)

## Can I sell digital downloads?

Yes. Add a product as a download and upload one file of up to 200 MB. Until Square Share checkout opens, the file reaches buyers from your own checkout or from you. [How do I sell digital downloads from my website?](/en/selling/sell-digital-downloads)

## Can I import my products?

Yes, from a CSV file of up to 2 MB, including a Shopify product export. Title and Price are the columns the import needs. [How do I add a product?](/en/products-and-storefronts/add-a-product)

## Do I have to charge VAT?

You are the seller, so VAT is yours to handle under your country's rules. Once your sales to consumers in other EU countries pass the EU threshold, the buyer's country's VAT applies. [What do I need to know about VAT as an EU seller?](/en/payments-and-payouts/vat-and-taxes)

## How do I refund a buyer?

Today, in the checkout tool the buyer paid with, or directly. Once Square Share checkout opens, the account owner refunds an order from Orders. [How do I refund a buyer?](/en/payments-and-payouts/refunds)

## Can I keep using Stripe Payment Links or Gumroad?

Yes. Paste the link as the product's purchase link, and buyers pay there as before. [Switching from Stripe Payment Links](/en/selling/switch-from-stripe-payment-links) and [switching from Gumroad](/en/selling/switch-from-gumroad).

## Can an AI assistant manage my store?

Yes. You can connect an AI assistant, such as Claude, to your Square Share account and choose what it may do. [How do I connect an AI assistant to Square Share?](/en/account/connect-ai-assistant)
