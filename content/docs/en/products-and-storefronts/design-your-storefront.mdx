<AnswerBlock>

Go to Storefront and click New storefront. Pick one of three looks, add your products to the grid with Add product, and drag their edges to resize them. Turn on Show a product page so tiles open each product's page, then save. The designer does not save on its own.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I create a storefront?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Storefront** and click **New storefront**. The designer opens.
2. Give it a name in the **Storefront name** field at the top. Until you do, it is called "Untitled storefront".
3. Pick a look (see below).
4. Click **Add product** in the toolbar and choose a product. It lands on the grid as one cell.
5. Drag a tile's edges to make it bigger. Repeat for every product you want to show.
6. Open the **Product page** settings and turn on **Show a product page**.
7. Click **Save**, or press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on a Mac). The dashboard confirms with "Storefront saved."

</Steps>

If you leave with changes that are not saved, the designer asks "Save your changes?" and offers **Save changes**, **Discard changes** or **Keep editing**. While something is not saved, the header shows "Unsaved changes".

## Which look should I pick?

A look sets the colours, font, corners, spacing and what a tile shows, for the whole storefront at once. Your layout, your products and any tile you styled by hand stay as they are, so you can switch looks at any time.

| Look | What a tile shows |
| --- | --- |
| Minimal | Pictures only. Name and price on hover. |
| Classic | Name and price under every picture. |
| Bold | Price always on the picture, name on hover. |

## What can I change after picking a look?

The settings are grouped like this in the designer:

| Group | What you set there |
| --- | --- |
| Theme | Background, accent colour, grid spacing (0 to 32 px) and canvas size |
| Header | The storefront name and bio shown above the grid |
| Typography | The font. Upload your own as WOFF2, WOFF, TTF or OTF, up to 2 MB |
| Product cards | Corner roundness, where the title and price sit, the price tag style |
| Sold out | How products that have run out are shown |
| Product page | **Show a product page** and **Allow search engines** |

Blocks can also be rotated (from -180 to 180 degrees). Beside products you can add shapes and your own uploaded images from the **Shapes** and **Uploads** panels. The **Layers** panel lists every block so you can change which sits on top.

## How big can a storefront be?

| Setting | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Canvas width | 3 to 12 columns |
| Canvas height | 2 to 60 rows |
| Blocks on one storefront | Up to 120 |

On a phone, the grid reflows its tiles to fit the narrow screen.

## What do Show a product page and Allow search engines do?

**Show a product page** decides whether tiles lead anywhere. On: tapping a tile opens that product's page, and the storefront gets a page of its own to share. Off: tiles open nothing, and the storefront's own link stops working too. A product's page only goes live with this switch on. See [Why is my product page not live?](/en/troubleshooting/product-page-not-live).

**Allow search engines** decides whether those pages can appear in search results. It starts off. Off keeps your product pages out of search results, and anyone with a link can still open them. Turn it on if you want people to find your products through Google and other search engines.
