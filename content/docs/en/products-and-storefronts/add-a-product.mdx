<AnswerBlock>

In the dashboard, go to Products and click Add product. A title and a price are enough to save it. Choose whether you ship it or sell it as a download, set it to Active, and save. Buyers can see it once you place it on a storefront that shows product pages.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I add a product?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products** and click **Add product**. The page that opens is called "New product".
2. Under **Basics**, type a **Title** and a price, and choose the currency (EUR or USD). Add a **Description** and a display image if you have them.
3. Under **What are you selling?**, choose **Something I ship** or **A download**. A download needs its file, up to 200 MB.
4. Under **Visibility**, choose **Active** if buyers should be able to buy it, or **Draft** to keep it hidden while you work on it.
5. Click **Save product**.

</Steps>

The product does not have a page yet. Put it on a storefront, with product pages turned on, to give it one. See [How do I design my storefront?](/en/products-and-storefronts/design-your-storefront).

## What is the difference between Active and Draft?

| Status | Who sees it | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active | Buyers, once it is on a storefront | The product is ready to sell |
| Draft | Only you | You are still writing it, or it is not for sale right now |

Setting a product back to Draft takes its page offline. The **Get set up** card on the Overview page brings back its publish step if that was your only live product.

## What else can I add?

Everything below **Basics** is optional. The form calls these extras "Make it shine": add whatever helps buyers decide.

| Section | What it holds | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock | Unlimited, or a set number that counts down to zero, with a low-stock alert and a maximum per order | Up to 100 per order |
| Options | Sizes, colours or versions, shown as swatches, chips or a dropdown | 4 groups, 48 options in total |
| Photos | More angles and close-ups. A photo can belong to one option | 24 extra photos, 10 MB each |
| Badges | Short labels such as "Handmade", beside the product or on its photo | 6 badges, 3 on the photo |
| Specifications | Dimensions, weight, materials, care, what is included | 30 rows |
| Documents | Manuals and certificates as PDF files | 8 PDFs, 20 MB each |
| Safety and compliance | The maker's name, address and email, for physical goods | Hidden for downloads |
| Add to order | Up to four of your other products, of the same kind and currency, to buy in the same order | Buyers do not see it yet: it needs Square Share checkout, which is not available |

Images can be PNG, JPG, WEBP, GIF or AVIF, up to 10 MB each.

The dashboard explains the Safety and compliance section this way: EU product-safety law asks for the maker's details on physical goods. If the maker is outside the EU, there is also room for the EU responsible person. What each field is for: [What product safety information does the EU require online?](/en/products-and-storefronts/product-safety-information)

## How do buyers pay for it?

The button on the product page depends on what you set:

| What you set | The button | Where it goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A purchase link on the product | "Checkout on" and the link's site name | Your own checkout page, in a new tab |
| No purchase link | **Ask about this product** | An email to your contact email, with the product title in the subject |

To add a purchase link, open **Selling through your own checkout? Add its link** on the product form. The link must start with `https://`.

## Can I import products from another platform?

Yes, from a CSV file. Go to **Products** and click **Import**. It works with a Shopify product export or any tool that exports a CSV, up to 2 MB per file.

- You match the file's columns to Title, Description, Price, SKU and Stock. Title and Price are needed.
- One currency applies to every product in the file.
- Shopify's variant rows are folded into one product each. Add sizes and colours after importing.
- Photos are not imported. Add them to each product afterwards.
- You choose to import them as **Drafts** or as **Live products**. Live products need your [seller details](/en/seller-details) first.

You see exactly which rows will be imported, and which skipped, before anything is saved. Every skip reason and its fix: [Why did my CSV import skip rows?](/en/troubleshooting/csv-import-problems)
