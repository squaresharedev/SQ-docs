<AnswerBlock>

Go to Settings, then Shipping & returns. Say where you ship from, how fast you dispatch, where you deliver and how long it takes, and what you offer on returns. Square Share turns your answers into a Shipping and a Returns section on every product page. Until you answer, product pages show neither section.

</AnswerBlock>

## How do I set them?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Settings** and choose **Shipping & returns**.
2. Check **Ships from**. It starts as the country in your [seller details](/en/seller-details).
3. Type a **Dispatch time**, for example "Ships within 1-3 business days".
4. Under **Where you ship, and how long it takes**, click **Add a destination** for each area you ship to, with its delivery time and, if you want to show it, its shipping cost.
5. Under **Returns**, pick a **Returns window** and who pays **Return postage**. Add any **Exceptions**.
6. Check **What buyers will read**, then save. The dashboard confirms with "Shipping & returns saved."

</Steps>

You set this once for your whole account. It applies to every product that ships, on every storefront.

## Where does each answer show up?

| You fill in | Buyers see |
| --- | --- |
| Ships from | "Ships from" and the country, in the Shipping section |
| Dispatch time | One line beside the button on the product page |
| Destinations | Each area with its delivery time, and its cost if you gave one |
| Anything else about shipping | Your note, in the Shipping section |
| Returns window and Return postage | A sentence such as "Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery. Return postage is paid by the buyer." |
| Exceptions | Your text, in the Returns section |

The **What buyers will read** box shows the finished text as you type.

## How many destinations should I add?

Group them the way you really ship. One row for your own country, one for the rest of the EU and one for everywhere else is plenty for most sellers. You can add up to six.

Leave the cost blank if it varies. A cost is charged once per order, whatever the quantity or weight: buyers order one product at a time, because there is no cart yet.

## What does the returns window mean?

It is what you offer on top of the rights EU buyers already have. The choices are "None beyond the statutory right", 14 days (marked as the EU statutory minimum), 30 days, or a number of days you choose.

Your product pages already state the EU rights for buyers at a distance: 14 days to cancel without giving a reason, unless a legal exception applies (for example made-to-order or sealed goods), and a guarantee of at least two years that goods match what was sold. Choosing "None beyond the statutory right" does not remove those rights.

Use **Exceptions** for anything the window does not cover, such as made-to-order pieces. Buyers read it before they buy.

These are general points about what the dashboard shows, not legal advice. If you are unsure which rules apply to what you sell, check with an adviser in your country.

## Can I write the text myself?

Yes. Under **Write it yourself instead**, type your own shipping policy, returns policy, or both. Anything you put there replaces the generated text word for word. Leave a box blank to keep using the answers above.

## What if some products ship differently?

Create a shipping profile, for example "Bulky items", under **Shipping profiles**. A profile can have its own dispatch time, its own shipping text and its own cost. Then open each of those products, and pick the profile in the product's **Shipping** section. Every other product keeps your usual terms. You can have up to eight profiles.

Downloads have no shipping section.
