<AnswerBlock>

Since 13 December 2024, the EU General Product Safety Regulation asks online offers of consumer products to show the manufacturer's name and contact details, an EU responsible person when the manufacturer is outside the EU, details that identify the product, and any warnings. A Square Share product's Safety and compliance section holds each of these.

</AnswerBlock>

This page explains what the regulation lists and where it goes in Square Share. It is not legal advice.

## What does the regulation ask an online offer to show?

Article 19 of [Regulation (EU) 2023/988](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/988/oj) lists four things. Each has a place in the product form:

| What Article 19 lists | Where it goes in Square Share |
| --- | --- |
| The manufacturer's name, registered trade name or trade mark, and a postal and electronic address | **Manufacturer**, **Manufacturer address**, **Manufacturer email** |
| If the manufacturer is not established in the EU: the name, postal and electronic address of the responsible person in the EU | **EU responsible person**, **Responsible person address**, **Responsible person email** |
| Information that identifies the product, including a picture, its type and any other identifier | Your product photos, and **Type, batch or serial number** |
| Warnings or safety information, in a language consumers can easily understand | **Warnings and safety information** |

## How do I fill in the Safety and compliance section?

<Steps>

1. In the dashboard, open **Products** and open the product.
2. Go to **Safety and compliance**. The dashboard describes it as: "Who made it and how to reach them. EU product-safety law asks for this on physical goods."
3. Fill in **Manufacturer**, **Manufacturer address** and **Manufacturer email**.
4. If the manufacturer is outside the EU, fill in the three responsible person fields. The form's hint: "Only when the manufacturer is outside the EU."
5. Add the **Type, batch or serial number** and any **Warnings and safety information**.
6. Save the product.

</Steps>

Once you fill in any part of the section, the three manufacturer fields become required.

| Field | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Manufacturer, EU responsible person | 120 characters |
| Manufacturer address, Responsible person address | 300 characters |
| Type, batch or serial number | 80 characters |
| Warnings and safety information | 2,000 characters |

## I make the products myself. Who is the manufacturer?

You are. The regulation treats whoever makes a product, or has it made, and sells it under their own name or trade mark as its manufacturer. Enter your own trader name, postal address and an email you read.

## What do buyers see?

A **Safety and compliance** section on the product page, with the manufacturer's details, the responsible person in the EU when you added one, the product identifier, and your warnings under **Warnings**.

## Does this apply to downloads?

Square Share hides the section for products sold as a download. The dashboard's note on downloads says: "A download needs no shipping or product-safety details."

## Does it apply to everything I sell?

The regulation covers new and second-hand consumer products supplied in the course of a commercial activity. Some products, such as food and medicines, fall under their own rules instead. Whether your own sales count as a commercial activity depends on your situation. The EU's official [Your Europe page on selling online](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/selling-in-eu/selling-goods-services/ecommerce-distance-selling/index_en.htm) is a starting point.
